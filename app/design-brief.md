# Flightpath — design brief

## Design read
For disc golf fans who treat the DGPT like a living ledger: sharp, athletic, calm confidence — not crypto neon, not stadium chrome.

## Concept spine
**The flight ledger** — every throw writes a line; the site reads like a season-long scorecard that opens into player dossiers.

## Delivery tier
`cinema` — Lenis + GSAP, scroll chapters, interactive hero depth.

## Locked palette
- Ground chalk: `#F2F5F0`
- Ink: `#121816`
- Pine: `#16382C`
- Mist: `#D7E3DA`
- Flight lime (one accent): `#C6E85A`
Defense: fairway greens + chalk paper + a single fluorescent flight trail. Avoids purple/Aave glow, graphite+orange, and neon-cyan-on-black.

## Locked type
**Syne** (display) + **IBM Plex Sans** (body) + **IBM Plex Mono** (stats). Athletic geometry without Inter-as-display.

## Tier-1 technique
**B1 — Cutout parallax rig** on the hero course plate: basket/disc layers drift on scroll + subtle cursor. Enacts the flight-ledger spine by making depth feel like a throw through the fairway.

## Section plan
1. Hero — full-bleed plate + brand + one line + CTA (split)
2. Featured players — 3-col Mobbin/Aave news-card grid (unique family)
3. Elite Series schedule — horizontal date rail
4. Player directory — vertical Recent-Posts list with filter pills
5. Closing CTA — single statement + link to PDGA Elite Series

## Asset plan
Hero course plate, 3 card gradient plates, monogram/favicon, OG card. Player cards use generated gradient plates + initials (no fake headshots of real athletes).

## CTA inventory
- `EnterFlight` — lime fill, magnetic hover (hero)
- `ViewRoster` — ghost ink underline arrow (section headers)
- `OpenDossier` — card-as-button, lift + lime edge (player cards)
